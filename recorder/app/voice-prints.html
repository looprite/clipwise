Recognise voices (off)

When this is on, Clipwise keeps a voice print (a set of numbers made from a person's voice) for each person you name on a call, and one for you from your microphone. Prints stay on this Mac. They are never sent to the Clipwise server or your team. For now Clipwise only measures how well it would recognise people; it doesn't name anyone yet.

A voice print is a biometric identifier. Some states and countries, Illinois for one, require each person's written consent before their voice print is kept. You're responsible for knowing the recording and biometric laws where you record, and for getting consent where it's needed.

You can delete one person's print here at any time. Turn this off to delete every voice print Clipwise has kept for recognising people. Each recording also keeps its own voice measurements until you delete that recording.
