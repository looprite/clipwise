Use Clipwise in Claude

Ask Claude about your calls. Add this address to Claude as a custom connector:

In Claude:

  1. Go to Customize, then Connectors, and click Add.
  2. Hover over Custom, then choose Web.
  3. Name it Clipwise, paste the address above, and click Continue.
  4. Under Authentication, choose Sign in now, then click Add. Sign in with Google when Clipwise asks.
  5. In a chat, click + (or type /), hover over Connectors, and turn Clipwise on.

To try it, ask: “What changed in the Panfold project between the two calls, and is anyone behind on what they committed to?”

The two demo calls are made-up samples in your recordings, visible only to you. You can remove them any time from your sign-in page.
